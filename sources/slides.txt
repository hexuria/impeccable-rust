QA
So, (some of) what it takes:
 -   Test exhaustively for all the things you haven’t thought of.
 -   Have trustworthy, realistic benchmarks for everything.
 -   Ensure past knowledge is not lost.
 -   Make it unlikely for developers to write incorrect code.
 -   Educate on and manage backwards compatibility.
 -   Track dependencies thoroughly.
 -   Make stagnation a recurrent choice.
